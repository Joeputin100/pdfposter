Delete Your Account & Data

You can request deletion of your Poster PDF account and its associated data at any time. This applies whether or not you still have the app installed.

How to request deletion

  1. Email joeputin100@gmail.com with the subject line “Delete my Poster PDF account”.
  2. Send the request from the Google account email address you used to sign in, so we can verify ownership.
Ready now? Send the deletion request

What gets deleted

  • Your account record (Google account identifier and authentication metadata)
  • Usage counters, generation/upscale history, and history metadata
  • Credit balance and credit log (unused purchased credits are forfeited on deletion — contact us first with any refund questions)
  • Any images or upscale outputs stored in your cloud storage area

What we may retain

  • Purchase and transaction records required for tax, billing reconciliation, and abuse prevention (retained only as long as legally necessary)

Timeline

We confirm and complete deletion requests within 30 days. Files stored only on your device (generated PDFs, local caches) are yours — deleting the app or clearing its storage removes them.
