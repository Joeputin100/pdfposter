Print it huge. Poster PDF turns any picture on your phone into a print-ready poster — tiled across ordinary printer pages you tape together, or exported as one large-format PDF for a print shop.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Pick any image — a photo, artwork, a kid's drawing.
2. Choose your poster size: from two pages to wall-sized.
3. Poster PDF slices it into perfectly aligned, overlapping tiles with trim guides.
4. Print at home, trim, tape, done.

AI UPSCALING, DONE RIGHT
Small images look blurry when printed big. Poster PDF fixes that:
• On-device AI upscaling — free, private, works offline, GPU-accelerated.
• Cloud AI upscaling — pro-grade models (including Topaz-class enhancement) for the sharpest large prints, with a live time estimate before you commit.
• Always lossless: every AI result is delivered as pixel-perfect PNG, never recompressed.

BUILT FOR REAL PRINTING
• Trim marks, overlap margins, and page-count preview before you print.
• Handles very large images without running out of memory — 17+ megapixel posters stream to disk as they render.
• Works with any printer that can print a normal page.

FAIR PRICING
The poster maker is free. On-device AI upscaling is free. Cloud AI upscaling uses simple prepaid credits — no subscription, no ads, and prices are shown up front in your currency. One credit is one cent.

ASK THE ASSISTANT
Not sure which upscale model or poster size to pick? The built-in AI assistant answers in plain language — type or speak your question.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your images stay on your device unless you choose cloud upscaling; cloud copies are stored in your own private area and can be deleted anytime. No ads, no data sold. Privacy policy: https://posterpdf.web.app/privacy-policy
